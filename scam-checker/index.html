Free scam checker: is this text or email a scam?

Got a text, email or pop-up that feels off? Paste it below. The check looks for the warning signs scammers rely on and tells you what to do next. It's free, and nothing you paste leaves your device.

Family Shield free check

Paste a message to check it

Copy the text, email or pop-up wording and paste it below. The check runs only in your browser: what you paste is never sent or saved anywhere. Leave out names, account numbers and codes anyway.

    What to do:

    This is advice from a set of rules, not a promise. It can miss a new trick or flag a real message. When money, a code or a login is involved, check it the direct way no matter what it says here.

    What it looks for

    • Pressure: deadlines, threats, late fees, "final notice".
    • Look-alike links: a company's name inside a different web address, shortened links, odd endings.
    • Risky requests: gift cards, crypto, wire transfers, reading back a code, confirming your password.
    • Common setups: "new number" family texts, unpaid tolls, stuck packages, fake fraud alerts, virus pop-ups, prizes you didn't enter.

    What it can't do

    It reads the words you paste against a set of rules. It doesn't open links, look anything up or know who really sent the message, so it can miss a new trick or flag a real message. Treat the result as a second opinion, not a promise. When money, a code or a login is involved, don't tap, go direct: open the company's app, type its website yourself, or call a number you find yourself.
