Services

The exact work for your project is whatever we agree in writing in your quote, which takes priority over the general descriptions on the site.

Quotes and pricing

Prices on the site are "Starting at" prices. They're a guide, not a quote. Before any work starts, we go over what you need and I send you a written quote with the scope and the final price. Work starts only after you accept that quote in writing (email is fine). If the scope changes along the way, I'll tell you what it means for the price and timing, and we agree any change in writing before I do the extra work.

Scope and acceptance

Your written quote lists what's included, what isn't, what I need from you, and the checks we'll use to confirm the work is done (for example, "a test request sent through your form appears in the shared list and alerts the right person"). Before anything goes live, we run those checks together.

After handoff, you have 5 business days to tell me if a check in your quote doesn't pass. I'll fix anything that's within the agreed scope at no extra charge. New requests or changes beyond the quote are quoted separately. The work counts as accepted when you confirm it, when you start using it for real, or when 5 business days pass after handoff without a reported problem, whichever comes first.

Monthly support plan

Unless your written plan says otherwise, the Monthly Remote Support Plan covers up to 3 small requests a month, each taking about 30 minutes or less. Unused requests don't roll over. Requests come in by email, and I send a first reply by the end of the next business day (Monday to Friday, Alaska time). The plan doesn't include 24/7, emergency, after-hours or on-site cover. For an outage or security problem, contact the provider (for example Google or Microsoft) first, and I'll help as soon as I can.

Bigger requests, new systems, migrations, hardware and systems I haven't set up or reviewed aren't covered. If a request goes beyond the plan, I'll say so and quote it before doing the work, or we can move it to the next month. I'll confirm your plan's limits in writing before you pay. You can cancel any time, and the plan runs to the end of the month you've paid for.

Payment

For projects over $249, 50% is due before work starts and the other 50% when you accept the finished work (see Scope and acceptance), unless your written quote says otherwise. Other payment terms, such as due dates and how to pay, are set out in your written quote or invoice. Please pay invoices by the due date shown. For a monthly plan, the price and billing schedule are in the plan we agree in writing. I may pause work on a project while a payment that's due is outstanding.

  • Give me the access I need (accounts, admin rights, devices or a screen-share session) when it's needed, and remove access I no longer need when the work is done.

  • Keep your own passwords and recovery details safe, and change any shared ones when the work is finished.
