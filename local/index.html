What we check

Six things, one visit

We go through each one with you and fix what we can on the spot.

01

Wi-Fi and router

Speed check, strong admin and Wi-Fi passwords, router updates and a separate guest network.

02

Backups

Confirm your important files are actually being backed up, and test that a file can be restored.

03

Passwords and 2-step sign-in

Turn on a second login step for email, banking and your key accounts, and tidy up shared passwords.

04

Email security

Check and fix the records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) that make it harder for scammers to fake email from your business.

05

Printers and slow computers

Updates, cleanup and the small fixes that eat everyone's time.

06

A one-page report

What we checked, what we fixed, and a short list of what to do next, in order of importance.
