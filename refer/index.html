Refer a business

Nov32026

Program terms last updated November 3, 2026.

  • 3 sections

On this page

  1. How it works
  2. The rewards
  3. The fine print

Small businesses trust other small businesses. If NeonKey helped you, and you know someone who is fighting their email, Wi-Fi, passwords or front-office busywork, send them our way. When they become a client, you both get something.

How it works

  1. Get your code. Every client gets a personal referral code (like NK-SMITH) and link after their job. Lost it? Email hello@neonkey.tech.
  2. Share it. Send your link to a business owner you know, or just tell them to put your name or code in the "Referred by" box on our contact form.
  3. They become a client. Once they pay for their first job, your credit is added and we email you to say thanks.

I was referred, get in touch

The rewards

  • You get a $100 credit toward any future NeonKey invoice, or one free month of the Support Plan, your choice.
  • They get $50 off their first job.
  • No limit. Refer five businesses that become clients, earn five credits.

The fine print

  • A referral counts when a new business (not an existing or past NeonKey client) names you or uses your code before or at their first booking, and then pays for their first job.
  • Rewards are service credits, not cash. They have no cash value, can't be transferred and can't be combined into a refund.
  • Credits expire 12 months after they're issued. A credit can bring an invoice down to $0 but not below; any leftover stays on your account until it expires.
  • The $50 new-client discount applies once, to the first job, and can't be combined with other discounts.
  • If two people refer the same business, the first one named gets the credit.
  • If you share your link publicly (a post, a review, a group), please mention that you get a reward if they sign up. That's what honest referrals look like, and it follows the spirit of the FTC's Endorsement Guides (opens in new tab).
  • No spam, please. Don't send your link to people you don't know or buy ads on it.
  • We may change or end the program at any time. Credits already earned are honored until they expire.

Questions? Email hello@neonkey.tech.
