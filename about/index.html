About NeonKey

NeonKey is a tech brand about unlocking: making AI, gear, skills and the week's news easier to use, whatever your level.

  • Run by Michael Ream
  • AI · Gear · Skills · News
  • Reviews are never paid placements

Who's behind it

NeonKey is run by Michael Ream, who has spent years working hands-on in tech: setting up, maintaining and troubleshooting networks and communication systems, and keeping them running when it matters. He holds a certificate in Computer Installation and Repair Technician from the California Institute of Applied Technology and is now pursuing an Applied Bachelor's Degree in Cybersecurity there, earning an associate degree in Computer Information Systems along the way. That coursework rounds out his real-world experience with the wider picture of how businesses use and protect technology. He has also worked hands-on in Microsoft Azure, building virtual machines and virtual networks and organizing resources into clean, well-structured resource groups.

Outside of that, he builds. Homelab projects, AI and automation workflows, and remote IT setups for small offices all get built and documented here in the open, so you can see how things actually work, not just the polished result.

How NeonKey works

  • Honest by default: reviews are never paid placements, and sponsorships or free products are always disclosed.
  • Beginner-friendly: plain language first, details for those who want them.
  • No hype: no guarantees and no made-up numbers.
