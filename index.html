What it fixes

The work that quietly costs you customers

Small businesses rarely lose jobs on price alone. They lose them in the gaps between the inbox, the spreadsheet and a busy week.

  • 01

    Missed inquiries

    A website form lands in an inbox nobody checks over the weekend. By Monday, they have called someone else.

  • 02

    Slow follow-up

    You sent the estimate. They meant to reply. Without a polite nudge, a quiet quote tends to stay quiet.

  • 03

    Repetitive admin

    Copying details from emails into a spreadsheet, typing the same answers, chasing the same invoice again.

  • 04

    Disconnected tools

    The form, the inbox, the calendar and the spreadsheet don't talk to each other, so things fall between them.

  • 05

    Weak IT and security

    Shared passwords, a backup nobody has tested, and business email a scammer can fake from your own domain.

Common questions

What does it cost?
The Follow-Up Desk is $99/month at the founding price (until Nov 30), then $250/month for new signups, after 14 days free. The Remote Small-Office IT Setup starts at $299, the Front Office Package is $999, and support is from $99/month. Custom work always gets a fixed written quote before anything starts. Software subscriptions are billed to you by those providers.
How long does it take?
The Follow-Up Desk takes about five minutes to set up yourself. The Remote Small-Office IT Setup usually takes 1 to 2 weeks and the Front Office Package usually 3 to 5 weeks from when you approve the quote. Your quote gives the plan.
Is my data safe? Who has access?
You keep every login and admin account, and subscriptions stay in your name. We work in the admin pages you give us access to, and you can remove that access when the work is done. See the privacy page and what the Follow-Up Desk keeps.
Do I need to be technical?
No. Setups are done remotely by screen share and in your admin pages, then you get a short walkthrough and written notes in everyday words. The Follow-Up Desk is built to be run by a busy owner from a phone.
Can I trust the AI parts?
AI answers can be wrong, so every assistant is set up to hand off to a person when it is unsure, and it is tested with real example questions before it goes live. A person reviews every setup. The Follow-Up Desk sends only the messages you approve.
What happens after setup?
You can keep the Monthly Remote Support Plan for small changes and questions by email (no 24/7 or emergency cover), or not. The Front Office Package includes three months of it. You can read the refund and cancellation policy before you buy.
