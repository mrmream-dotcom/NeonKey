Quick Win + Tech Checkup

We take one boring, repeat task off your plate and set it up to run on its own.

Some examples of what we can automate:

  • New website messages saved to a spreadsheet, with an email alert so you never miss one
  • An instant "we got your message" reply to every new customer inquiry
  • Incoming emails sorted and labeled, so urgent ones stand out
  • Receipts and invoices filed in folders, with the totals added to a spreadsheet
  • Reminder emails sent to customers the day before their appointment
  • A weekly summary of new leads or orders sent to your inbox

Remote Small-Office IT Setup

We get your office tech set up properly, all done remotely.

What that covers:

  • A professional email address with your business name (you@yourbusiness.com)
  • Settings that help your emails stay out of spam and stop scammers pretending to be you
  • Safer Wi-Fi: new passwords, router updates and a separate network for guests
  • Automatic backups of your important files, tested so you know they work
  • A password manager for your team, plus a code on your phone for signing in to key accounts
  • Simple checklists for setting up a new laptop or phone, and for when someone leaves

Done over video and screen share. You keep every login, and you get notes on everything we changed. Full details →
