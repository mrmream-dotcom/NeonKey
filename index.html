How it works

Scoped, built after hours, handed over to you

  1. Scope: we agree on what "done" looks like, by message or a short call. You get a written quote before any work starts.
  2. Build: setup and changeovers happen after 4 PM Alaska time or on weekends, so your staff don't lose a working hour.
  3. Check: we test it with you on screen: email records, sign-ins, a backup restore.
  4. Hand off: every account is created in your name. You get notes and a walkthrough, and you can switch off our access.
