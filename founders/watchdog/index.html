Watch it for me

Your domain passes today. Then someone adds a new sending tool, a record gets deleted, or a certificate quietly expires, and mail starts landing in spam before anyone notices. Founder Watchdog is a service we're thinking about building that would check your domain every day and email you when something changes.

  • Waitlist only
  • Nothing to pay
  • No commitment

What it would watch

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC: alert when a record disappears, breaks or loosens
  • Mail servers (MX) and DNS host (NS) changes
  • HTTPS certificate close to expiring
  • Domain registration close to expiring
  • Your homepage going down

Alerts would come by email, in everyday words, with what changed and how to fix it. One domain on the basic plan.

Planned price

$9 a month, or $90 a year. That's the price we'd plan to charge if we build it. It isn't on sale, and joining the list doesn't sign you up for anything.

Why we ask about DNS

Before Watchdog would watch a domain, the owner would add one short TXT record to prove it's theirs, so nobody can watch a domain that isn't theirs. If someone else manages your DNS, that's fine: they'd add it for you.

Honest status

  • It isn't built yet. We'll only build it if enough founders say they want it.
  • If we do, people on this list hear first, by one email. If we don't, we'll say so once and stop.
  • Want it fixed today instead? The free Launch Preflight checks your domain right now.
