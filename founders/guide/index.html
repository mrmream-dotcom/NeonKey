Questions

Do I need to be a developer?

No. You'll copy a few commands into a terminal and edit text files, and every step that needs code shows it.

Will it make me money?

It's a setup guide. It shows exactly how the systems are built; what they earn depends on your offer, your market and your work, so we don't promise results.

Are the tools free?

It names the tools we really use and has no affiliate links. Many have free tiers, but some (your domain and business email, for example) cost money and are billed by those companies, not included in the price of the guide. Check each tool's current pricing.

Is there setup help or support?

Self-serve only: no done-for-you setup or ongoing support. Questions about the guide itself are welcome at hello@neonkey.tech.

How is the included Email Authentication Setup delivered?

Enter your domain at checkout, or reply to the email your guide arrives in. We check your SPF, DKIM and DMARC and email you a report with the exact records to add, within one business day. You add the records in your DNS settings yourself; the report shows where. One domain per guide.
