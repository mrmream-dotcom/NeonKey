How the included Email Authentication Setup works

Enter your domain at checkout, or reply to the email your guide arrives in. We check your SPF, DKIM and DMARC and email you a report with the exact records to add, within one business day. You add the records in your DNS settings yourself; the report shows where. One domain per guide.
