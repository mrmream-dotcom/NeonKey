Every follow-up ends with one-tap buttons

  • Yes, let's go ahead. Your customer confirms on a short page (and can leave a phone number). You get an email right away, the job shows as Replied, and the follow-ups stop.
  • I have a question. They type it on the same kind of page, and it lands in your inbox with Reply going straight to them.
  • Pay the deposit. Shows only if you add your own payment link. It goes straight to your checkout, so the money goes to you, never through NeonKey. Invoices get "Pay the invoice" instead.

New inquiries get "Yes, let's set up a time". You can turn the buttons off in Settings.

Questions

When am I charged, and how do I cancel?

The first 14 days are free. Cancel before day 15 and you won't be charged. After that, cancel anytime from the billing link on your dashboard. Follow-ups stop when your subscription ends, and your list is deleted 30 days later.

When do follow-ups go out, and from whom?

Monday to Friday, between 9 AM and noon in your time zone, at most 60 a day. You pick the days (3, 7 and 14 by default) and write your own wording. Emails come from your business name at a NeonKey sending address, and replies reach your own inbox. You can get a copy of every follow-up.

Who can I add?

Only people who asked you for an estimate or are already your customers. It isn't for cold email or bought lists. Anyone can ask to stop with one click.

Does it connect to QuickBooks?

Not yet. You add estimates on the dashboard or paste an export from QuickBooks, Jobber or a spreadsheet.

Why not just use QuickBooks or Jobber reminders?

Those tools can send reminders too, and if they already do the job for you, keep using them. The Follow-Up Desk is for estimates that go quiet over email, and it's built around one rule: the moment a customer answers, the follow-ups stop and the answer comes to you. Out-of-office replies don't count as an answer, and a bad email address shows up in your Monday report.

Is it worth it?

Do the math with your own numbers: if your average job leaves you $1,250 after costs, one estimate that turns into work once a year pays for the Desk. That figure is an example, not an average.

Slipping through the cracks is common: when Harvard Business Review researchers sent a web inquiry to 2,241 US companies, 23% never answered at all. In Intuit QuickBooks' 2025 survey of 2,487 US small businesses, 56% were owed money on unpaid invoices, about $17,500 on average; the Desk has a polite reminder set for invoices, too.

Sources: Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011) (opens in new tab); Intuit QuickBooks 2025 Small Business Late Payments Report (opens in new tab). Results depend on your business. More follow-ups don't guarantee more jobs; what the Desk does is make sure nobody slips through because you were busy.

What if I haven't added an estimate yet?

New accounts get a short reminder on day 1, 3 and 10 if they haven't added an estimate yet, with a fresh link to the dashboard.
