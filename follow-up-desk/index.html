Stop letting estimates go quiet

Add the estimates you've sent. The Follow-Up Desk checks in with each customer for you, under your business name, and stops the moment they reply. You set it up yourself in about five minutes. No call needed.

How it works

  1. Start your free trial. 14 days free, card required, cancel anytime. You get a private link to your dashboard by email. There's no password to remember.
  2. Add your details. Your business name, the email replies should go to, and how you sign off. The three messages are written for you, and you can change every word.
  3. Add the estimates you've sent. One at a time, or paste a list exported from QuickBooks, Jobber or a spreadsheet.
  4. That's it. Follow-ups go out on weekday mornings, your time. When a customer replies, or taps "Yes, let's go ahead", you hear about it right away and that job's follow-ups stop on their own.

Why it's a good investment

  • One extra job can cover months of it. Do the math with your own numbers: if your average job leaves you $1,250 after costs, one estimate that turns into work once a year pays for the Desk. Bigger jobs pay it back faster.
  • Slipping through the cracks is common. When Harvard Business Review researchers sent a web inquiry to 2,241 US companies, 23% never answered at all. Usually that's because people are busy, not because they don't care.
  • Unpaid invoices hurt cash flow. In Intuit QuickBooks' 2025 survey of 2,487 US small businesses, 56% were owed money on unpaid invoices, about $17,500 on average. Businesses with overdue invoices were more likely to report cash-flow problems (50% vs 34%). The Desk has a polite reminder set for invoices, too.
  • It gives you time back. No more digging through sent mail to see who you haven't heard from. Follow-ups go out on their own, and you only step in when someone answers.
  • Low risk. Try it free for 14 days. After that it's monthly, cancel anytime, with no call or contract.

Sources: Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011) (opens in new tab); Intuit QuickBooks 2025 Small Business Late Payments Report (opens in new tab). The $1,250 figure is an example, not an average. Results depend on your business, and follow-ups can't promise replies, jobs or payments.

Why not just use QuickBooks or Jobber reminders?

Those tools can send reminders too, and if they already do the job for you, keep using them. The Follow-Up Desk is for estimates that go quiet over email, and it's built around one rule: the moment a customer answers, the follow-ups stop and the answer comes to you. Out-of-office replies don't count as an answer, a bad email address shows up in your Monday report, and anyone can ask to stop with one click.

Good to know

  • New accounts get a short reminder on day 1, 3 and 10 if they haven't added an estimate yet, with a fresh link to the dashboard.
  • Follow-ups go out Monday to Friday, between 9 AM and noon in your time zone, at most 60 a day.
  • You pick the days (3, 7 and 14 by default) and write your own wording. Send yourself a preview anytime.
  • Emails come from your business name at a NeonKey sending address, and replies reach your own inbox. You can get a copy of every follow-up.
  • Only add people who asked you for an estimate or are already your customers. It isn't for cold email or bought lists.
  • It doesn't connect to QuickBooks yet. You add estimates on the dashboard or paste an export.
  • More follow-ups don't guarantee more jobs. What it does is make sure nobody slips through because you were busy.
  • The first 14 days are free. Cancel before day 15 and you won't be charged. After that, cancel anytime from the billing link on your dashboard. Follow-ups stop when your subscription ends, and your list is deleted 30 days later.

Want it set up for you instead?

The Follow-Up Desk is do-it-yourself. If you'd rather have new requests from your website and inbox routed, answered with drafts and tracked on one list, that's the Front Office Package, a custom setup done with you.
