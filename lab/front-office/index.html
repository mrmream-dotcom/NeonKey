    What gets built

    The demo above is the Front Office Package, piece by piece:

    • One intake. Your website form, shared inbox and chat all feed one list, so nothing lives only in someone's head or phone.
    • An FAQ assistant. It answers the questions you get every day from your own content, and hands off to a person when it's unsure. See it working in the Office AI demo.
    • Routing rules. Each inquiry goes to the right person by service, location or source. Urgent ones alert the owner right away.
    • Replies you approve. Drafts come from templates you signed off on. A person presses send.
    • Follow-up reminders. A set schedule with a draft ready each time. It stops when the customer answers.
    • Email security. SPF, DKIM and DMARC checked and fixed, so scammers have a harder time faking your address.

    What you own at the end

    • The tracker, in your Google Sheet or customer database (CRM).
    • The templates and rules, written in your voice, which you can change yourself.
    • Every account and login. If we stop working together, it keeps running.
    • A walkthrough video and written notes, plus three months of the support plan while it beds in.
    • Room to grow. On the monthly plan we add or change features as you need them. Advanced features may cost extra, quoted first.

    How it gets built

    1. Scoping call. Where inquiries come from today, who handles what, and what should never be automated. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.
    2. Build in your accounts. Usually Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, a sheet or your CRM, and a mainstream automation tool or Google Apps Script.
    3. Test with fake leads. Every path gets tested before a real customer touches it, and you approve every template.
    4. Go live and hand off. We check the first real inquiries together, then you get the walkthrough and notes.

    Limitations

    • This page is a simulation that runs in your browser. The real version runs on your own tools, so it will look like your inbox and sheet, not this page.
    • AI drafts can be wrong. That's why a person approves every message and the assistant hands off when unsure.
    • It doesn't replace answering the phone. Calls still need a person or a separate phone setup.
    • Some tools need a paid plan at higher volume. Those subscriptions are billed to you by the provider, and we check that before quoting.
