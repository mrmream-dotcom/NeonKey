    A few things to know before you ask for a quote

    How do I know if I'm losing leads?

    Look back at last month and check three things: how long it took to send the first reply to each new inquiry, how many quotes got no follow-up after the first message, and how many past customers you haven't contacted in a year. If any answer surprises you, that's the place to start. Tell us what you find and we'll suggest the smallest fix that helps.

    Does anything reply to my customers without me?

    It depends on which one you pick.

    Front Office Package: only the FAQ assistant replies on its own, and only with answers you approved in advance, like hours or service area. Anything it isn't sure about goes to a person. Quote replies and follow-ups are drafted for you, and a person on your side sends them.

    Follow-Up Desk: yes, that's the point of it. Follow-ups on the estimates you add go out on their own, in wording you write and can edit, and they stop the moment the customer replies. You can send yourself a preview first and stop any job with one click. Compare them.

    Are there software costs on top?

    Where possible we build on tools you already pay for, like your email and a spreadsheet. If a setup needs a paid tool, its monthly fee is listed in your written quote before you decide, and the provider bills you directly.

    Why not just buy software?

    Sometimes you should. If an off-the-shelf app already does exactly what you need, use it, and I'm happy to help you set it up. A custom build makes sense when an app would make you change how you work: it fits the process you already have, plugs into the tools you already use, and you own it outright. You control where your data goes.

    How do you handle my accounts and data?

    Your accounts stay yours. You keep every admin login, and where a tool allows it I work from a separate admin account or delegated access that you can switch off, rather than your own password. I only open the settings and files the job needs. At handoff we review who has access, and you can remove mine (I'll show you how). I don't keep copies of your files or passwords after the work is done.

    Can I start small and add more later?

    Yes, that's how most people start. Begin with one fix or one module. Anything you add later is built to fit what's already there, and on the monthly plan simple changes are part of the plan. Advanced features may cost extra, and you always get the price before any work starts.
