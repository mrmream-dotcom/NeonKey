For small businesses that get regular customer questions and new inquiries from more than one place, and want the whole front office handled in one go: answering, capturing, routing, following up and keeping email secure. Remote Small-Office IT Setup

Help your emails reach inboxes instead of spam, make it harder for scammers to fake your domain, and get your Wi-Fi, backups and passwords locked down.

For small offices and solo pros. Get email, Wi-Fi, backups and passwords set up properly for a small office or solo business, fully remote.

  • Business email on your own domain (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365)
  • Email security settings that stop scammers faking your address (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Wi-Fi and router security walkthrough
  • Backups, password manager rollout and device checklists
See details

Who it's for

For small offices and solo pros, roughly 1 to 10 people, whose email, passwords and backups grew up by accident. It gets the basics set up properly, on accounts you own, without hiring an IT department.

What's included

  • Business email on your own domain in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365: mailboxes, extra addresses that land in the same inbox (aliases, like hello@ or billing@) and shared calendars
  • Email security settings published for your domain, so scammers can't easily fake your address (SPF, DKIM and DMARC records), with DMARC started in report-only mode (monitor mode)
  • A guided video session on your router: its built-in software (firmware) updates, admin and Wi-Fi passwords, modern Wi-Fi encryption (WPA2/WPA3) and a guest network
  • A simple backup plan for the files that matter, with a test restore
  • A business password manager set up with shared password folders (vaults), plus a second login step, like a code on your phone (2FA), on key accounts
  • Written checklists for setting up and retiring laptops and phones
  • Clear notes on every change, and admin access that stays with you

How it works

  1. Scoping call: A short video call to list what you have today and pick what is in scope. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.
  2. Setup sessions: I do the work remotely by screen share and in the admin settings pages (admin consoles) you give me access to. Some steps, like changes to your domain settings (DNS), can take a day or two to settle.
  3. Check it works: We send test emails, check the authentication records, try signing in with the second login step (2FA) and do a test restore from backup.
  4. Walkthrough and handoff: A short walkthrough of what changed, the written notes and checklists, and a review that you hold every admin login.

What you provide

  • Admin access to the company where you bought your domain (registrar) or where its settings are managed (DNS host), and to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 if you already use one
  • A list of the people, mailboxes and devices to include
  • Someone at the office who can join a video call and follow along on the router and devices

Works with

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, common business password managers (for example Bitwarden or 1Password), mainstream cloud backup tools, and consumer or prosumer routers you already own. Other tools are checked on the scoping call.

Typical turnaround

Usually 1 to 2 weeks from the scoping call, depending on scope and how quickly sessions can be booked. Your quote gives the plan.

What affects the final price

  • How many people, mailboxes and devices are included
  • Moving existing email from another provider
  • How many of the setup items you need
  • Extra domains, or cleanup of an old or messy setup

Software subscriptions (for example Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or a password manager) are billed to you directly by those providers and are not part of this price.

Questions

FAQ

Why not just buy software?

Sometimes you should. If an off-the-shelf app already does exactly what you need, use it, and I'm happy to help you set it up. A custom build makes sense when an app would make you change how you work. It fits the process you already have: your categories, your spreadsheet, your steps. It can also connect or replace subscriptions that overlap, and it plugs into the tools you already use. And you own it outright, so there are no surprise price hikes or features that disappear in an update. You control where your data goes. If you'd like, I can maintain it for you, or you can run it yourself.

How do you handle my accounts and data?

Your accounts stay yours. You keep every admin login, and where a tool allows it I work from a separate admin account or delegated access that you can switch off, rather than your own password. I only open the settings and files the job needs. At handoff we review who has access, and you can remove mine (I'll show you how). If you're on a support plan, my access stays only for the systems the plan covers. I don't keep copies of your files or passwords after the work is done. Questions about this are welcome on the scoping call.
