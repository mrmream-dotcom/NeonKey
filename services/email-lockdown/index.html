Email Lockdown

Make it harder for scammers to send email pretending to be you, and help your real email land in inboxes. $49 flat, no call needed.

What you get

  • Your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records checked (these are the settings that prove your email is really from you)
  • The exact records your domain needs, written for your email provider and the other tools that send email as you
  • Either step-by-step instructions for your domain host, or we add them for you if you invite us as a collaborator
  • A fake-email policy (DMARC) that starts gently: it reports mail faking your address before anything gets blocked
  • A branded report with your score and findings, plus rechecks on day 2 and day 5
  • A short note of what changed and what to watch for

Good to know

  • It's quick. You type your domain at checkout, we check its public records, and your report with the exact records usually arrives by email within a few minutes. If your setup needs a closer look by a person, it can take up to 1 business day.
  • No passwords needed. Your public records are visible to anyone, so we can check them without logging in.
  • DKIM keys are created in your email provider's admin settings (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365). If you're not the admin, your admin may need to click one button. We'll show you where.
  • One domain per purchase. Moving email to a new provider or a messy multi-domain setup is a bigger job, and we'll tell you before doing anything more.
  • Already protected? If your three records are already in place, your report says so and covers extra checks beyond the basics, with the next useful step if there is one. That's still a full report, so the sale is final once it's sent.

What "all set" means

When your report shows 4 of 4, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are published for your domain and pass our check of your public records on the day we checked. Inboxes can then confirm that mail claiming to be from you really is. It doesn't stop scam emails sent to you, it can't make every message land in the inbox, and DMARC starts in monitor mode, so it reports fakes before anything gets blocked. When you add a new tool that sends email as you, like a newsletter or invoicing app, turn on its "authenticate your domain" setting.

What we keep

Your order record (name, email, business name, domain and check results) is kept for 180 days so we can run your rechecks and answer questions, then it deletes automatically. Copies of the emails we send you stay in our business inbox as records. Your card details stay with Stripe and never reach us. See the privacy policy.
