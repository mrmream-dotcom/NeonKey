Remote Small-Office IT Setup

Business email, email security, Wi-Fi, backups and passwords set up right, without hiring an IT department. Everything happens remotely by video call, screen share and written notes, on accounts you own.

Who it's for: Small offices and solo pros, roughly 1 to 10 people, whose email, passwords and backups grew up by accident.

  • Video call, screen share, email
  • Admin access stays with you

What you get

  • Business email on your own domain in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365: mailboxes, extra addresses that land in the same inbox (aliases, like hello@ or billing@) and shared calendars
  • Email security settings published for your domain, so scammers can't easily fake your address (SPF, DKIM and DMARC records), with DMARC started in report-only mode (monitor mode)
  • A guided video session on your router: its built-in software (firmware) updates, admin and Wi-Fi passwords, modern Wi-Fi encryption (WPA2/WPA3) and a guest network
  • A simple backup plan for the files that matter, with a test restore
  • A business password manager set up with shared password folders (vaults), plus a second login step, like a code on your phone (2FA), on key accounts
  • Written checklists for setting up and retiring laptops and phones
  • Clear notes on every change, and admin access that stays with you

Pick what you need

A scope menu. We agree what's in before the quote.

  • Business email & domain

    Set up Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 on your own domain: mailboxes, extra addresses like hello@ or billing@ that land in the same inbox (aliases), shared calendars and a clean admin account that you own.

  • Email security (authentication)

    Publish the email security settings that make it harder for scammers to fake your address (SPF, DKIM and DMARC records), so your mail is more likely to land in inboxes. Start DMARC in report-only mode (monitor mode), then tighten it once reports look clean.

  • Wi-Fi & router security walkthrough

    A guided video session on your own router: router software (firmware) updates, strong admin and Wi-Fi passwords, modern Wi-Fi encryption (WPA2/WPA3), a separate guest network and turning off features you don't use.

  • Backups

    A simple 3-2-1 plan (three copies, on two kinds of storage, one kept off-site) for the files that matter: what gets backed up, where, how often, and a test restore so you know it works.

  • Password manager rollout

    Pick a business password manager, set up shared password folders (vaults), turn on a second login step, like a code on your phone (2FA), for key accounts and give everyone a short how-to.

  • Device setup checklists

    A written checklist for new and departing laptops and phones: updates, disk encryption, screen lock, accounts in and out. Yours to reuse.

How the plan works, in detail

Who it's for

For offices that already have their basics set up and want someone to email when a small change or question comes up, without a big IT contract.

How it works

  1. Agree the plan: We confirm in writing how many requests are included each month (3 as standard) and the response window, before you pay.
  2. Send a request: Email one request at a time, describing what you need.
  3. Get it handled: I reply by the end of the next business day and make the change remotely where needed. If a request is bigger than a small one, I say so and quote it before doing the work.
  4. Review as you go: If your needs change, we adjust the plan. Cancel anytime.

What you provide

  • Admin access (or a delegated admin account) for the systems covered
  • Requests by email, with enough detail to act on

Works with

The same tools covered by the setup: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, your password manager, backups and devices. Usually offered after a Remote Small-Office IT Setup.

What affects the final price

  • How many people are covered
  • How many requests are included each month
  • The response window you choose

No 24/7, emergency or onsite cover. For an outage or a security problem, contact the provider (for example Google or Microsoft) first, and I'll help as soon as I can. Requests over about 30 minutes, new systems, migrations, hardware and systems I haven't set up or reviewed are scoped and quoted separately.

How a setup works

You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

  1. Scope it in writingBy email (or a short video call, if you prefer) we list what you have today and pick what is in scope. You get a fixed written quote before any work starts.
  2. Setup sessionsI do the work remotely by screen share and in the admin settings pages (admin consoles) you give me access to. Some steps, like changes to your domain settings (DNS), can take a day or two to settle.

Are software subscriptions included?

Software subscriptions (for example Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or a password manager) are billed to you directly by those providers and are not part of this price. Not included unless your quote says so: on-site visits, buying or repairing hardware, moving large file archives, and systems outside the agreed scope. Those are quoted separately.

What do I need to provide?
  • Admin access to the company where you bought your domain (registrar) or where its settings are managed (DNS host), and to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 if you already use one
  • A list of the people, mailboxes and devices to include
  • Someone at the office who can join a video call and follow along on the router and devices
How long does it take?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks from when you approve the quote, depending on scope and how quickly sessions can be booked. Your quote gives the plan.

What's not included?
  • Onsite visits or hardware installs
  • Government systems or contracts
  • Regulated data handling or compliance work (for example health records under HIPAA or card payments under PCI)
  • 24/7, emergency or after-hours response
  • Guarantees of security, uptime or deliverability

If something's outside this scope, I'll say so before quoting and point you to a better fit.
