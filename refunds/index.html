Refunds and cancellations

The short version for every NeonKey purchase. The Terms of Service are the official wording, and if anything here ever differs, the terms win.

Email a refund or cancel requestCancel a subscription yourself (opens in new tab)

How to ask

  1. Email hello@neonkey.tech from the address you paid with, or include it. The button above fills in the four things we need: your email, what you bought, what went wrong and what you'd like.
  2. We look up your order in Stripe by that email. You don't need an order number, and never send card details.
  3. You get an answer by email, usually within one business day (Monday to Friday).
  4. If a refund is approved, it goes back to the card you paid with. Stripe sends the refund right away; your bank usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Guides are emailed automatically after checkout and are final once sent. If your copy doesn't arrive within one business day, the file won't open, or there's a mistake in it, email us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you in full. Full terms.

Follow-Up Desk

  • Free trial: cancel before the 14 days are up and you're never charged.
  • Monthly plan: cancel anytime from the Billing button on your dashboard, or with the billing link (opens in new tab) above. Follow-ups keep running to the end of the month you've paid for; partial months aren't refunded.
  • Yearly plan: ask within 30 days of paying for a full refund. After that, unused months aren't refunded, and you can turn off the renewal anytime.
  • Our mistake: if the Desk fails to send follow-ups for a stretch because of a problem on our side, email us and we'll credit or refund that month.
  • Just need a break? The Pause button on your dashboard holds every follow-up until you press Resume.

Quoted projects

How the billing link works

The billing link is Stripe's secure customer page. Type the email you paid with, and Stripe emails you a one-time sign-in link. From there you can cancel a subscription, update your card or download receipts. NeonKey never sees your card number.
