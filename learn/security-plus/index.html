CompTIA Security+ study guide

CompTIA Security+ is an entry-level cybersecurity certification. This guide breaks the exam into its five domains and explains each one in plain language, with key terms and a short self-check on every page.

Exam version: these notes follow SY0-701 (V7). CompTIA plans to launch SY0-801 (V8) on or around November 17, 2026. SY0-701 stays available in English until June 11, 2027. Book the version you studied for.

Why Security+ matters

  • A common starting point. Security+ is a vendor-neutral certification. It covers core security skills instead of one company’s products, which is why it’s often listed for entry-level security and IT jobs, such as security analyst or systems administrator.
  • Used by the US Department of Defense. Security+ is one of the certifications that can meet the qualification requirements for several cybersecurity and IT work roles under the US DoD 8140 program.
  • A foundation for what comes next. The exam covers the basics most security work builds on: controls, threats, architecture, day-to-day operations, and risk and governance.

Is this for you?

Be realistic before you start:

  • Time. The exam has up to 90 questions (multiple choice plus hands-on “performance-based” questions) and you get 90 minutes. Most people study for weeks or months first.
  • Money. Each attempt needs a paid exam voucher. At the time of writing, CompTIA’s US store lists a voucher at around US$439, and a retake means buying another voucher. Check CompTIA for current pricing in your country.
  • Experience. CompTIA recommends having Network+ and two years of experience in a security or systems administrator role first. That’s a recommendation, not a requirement. If you’re brand new to IT, the networking and systems basics will help a lot.
  • No guarantees. These notes are a study aid. They don’t replace the official exam objectives and they can’t promise a pass. You need a scaled score of 750 (on a 100–900 scale).

The five domains

  1. Domain 1: General Security Concepts · 12% of SY0-701
  2. Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations · 22% of SY0-701
  3. Domain 3: Security Architecture · 18% of SY0-701
  4. Domain 4: Security Operations · 28% of SY0-701
  5. Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight · 20% of SY0-701

Also handy: the acronym list (Security+ uses a lot of them).

How to use this guide

  1. Read the notes for one domain. Each section is short on purpose.
  2. Go through the key-term cards. Try to say each definition out loud before you open the card.
  3. Do the self-check at the bottom. If you miss one, reread that section.
  4. Compare what you’ve covered against CompTIA’s official exam objectives, then practice with hands-on labs and full-length practice tests.
